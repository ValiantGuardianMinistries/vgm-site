## Agreed foundation
- Name: Valiant Guardian Kids (VGK), fallback after initial search found other Valiant Kids uses. No trademark/domain clearance has been claimed.
- Child-friendly, Christian adventure world with warm, friendly guardians. No chats, comments, messaging, public child profiles, public uploads, or multiplayer.
- Boys section on the left, Parents in the middle, Girls on the right; Both is the shared kids resource audience. No gender access restrictions.
- Every resource has a recommended minimum age: All ages / 3+ / 6+ / 8+ / 10+ / 12+ / 15+, plus an independent parent-involvement level: On your own / Parent nearby / Do together.
- Server-protected admin content publishing and real aggregate statistics; no individual child browsing histories.
- One shared website/app foundation. Initial owner-private test; public release is a separate decision.

## Current design direction
One long, joyful illustrated landscape containing all eight eras at once, with clickable destinations, gentle optional motion, and ordinary library navigation. This is map navigation, not Play Mode. Honor reduced-motion preferences and keep all destinations keyboard accessible.
Agreed growth timeline: Ancient—infants–2 (parent-led); Medieval—3–5; Feudal—6–7; Age of Sail—8–9; Frontier—10–11; Industrial—12–14; Modern—15–17; Future—All ages. These are browsing recommendations, not security gates; article ages remain independently assigned. Add 3+ and 6+ resource labels to support the younger destinations.

## Seven-day devotional rhythm
Adapt the actual adult devotional's weekly theme into language and actions children can use; do not invent its contents without the adult source. Scale challenges to ability (e.g. a small set of push-ups rather than the adult amount, with alternatives).
1. Heart — one specific Bible story and related character application.
2. Soul — one specific Bible story and spiritual practice.
3. Mind — one specific Bible story and thinking activity.
4. Strength — one specific Bible story and physical practice.
5. Community — one specific Bible story and loving action.
6. Father–son / Mother–daughter — paired gender-specific devotional/activity options; allow a trusted-caregiver adaptation.
7. Rest — rest, gratitude, and relaxed reflection.

### Agreed daily template
- Bible story: What happened?
- Big idea: What does this teach us about God and how we live?
- Real life: When might a kid face something similar?
- Challenge: What could someone actually do today?
- Discussion: What would you ask another kid?
- Prayer: What would you ask God to help with?

- **Historical fiction:** Guardians from the eight eras can be fictional characters in historical settings; clearly distinguish imagined narrative, verified historical context, and Bible stories.

- **Era-themed minigames:** Eight eras as game settings, practical activities, and character themes.
- **Live show:** Devotionals become script foundations for the Valiant Guardian Kids live show. Later add episodes within the site and choose appropriate video hosting.
- **Installable app / app stores:** Revisit after the website, sharing code, content, and data where practical.
- **Operations before broad release:** Team admin authorization if requested, backups, content review, larger video delivery, accessibility/security checks, hosting privacy/access settings, and ongoing maintenance.
