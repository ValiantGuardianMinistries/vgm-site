Higher Authority

We believe that one becomes all they can be when they willingly submit themselves to the Higher Authority. True strength is found in self-mastery through self-surrender to God.

Higher Authority exists to equip individuals to confidently embrace their identity, embody their convictions, and fulfill their calling.

We want those who wear our apparel to feel courageous, confident, and equipped to bring light into the darkness. Not because the clothing itself possesses power, but because it reminds the wearer of what they believe, whom they serve, and what they are called to do.

Every product tells a story. Every design carries meaning. Every piece is an outward expression of an inward conviction.

Submit to the Higher Authority. Become all you were created to be.
