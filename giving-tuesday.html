Why we exist

“We fight for our enemies so that they may become our brothers.”

Our mission is to unite deep biblical understanding with practical Christian living. The goal isn't simply more content—it is disciples who make disciples.

The mission

Know the Word.
Live the Word.

Valiant Guardian Ministries offers a modern discipleship approach to old-school Sunday school that bridges the gap for all generations. We focus on spiritual disciplines, classical virtues, and traditional values—helping people love God with all their heart, soul, mind, and strength.

01

Teach

Make biblical teaching approachable without losing its depth through lessons, videos, and study resources.

02

Form

Develop Christlike character through spiritual disciplines, biblical understanding, and practical obedience.

03

Multiply

Equip individuals, families, and churches to pass on what they learn and disciple others.

How donations will be used

Two priorities. One mission.

Our first priority is production and studio equipment. After immediate equipment needs are met, additional gifts will help support everyday ministry operations. Purchases will be prioritized according to funds actually available.

01

Equip the Mission

Increase the consistency and quality of biblical content while giving volunteers tools to help produce it.

  • Three refurbished Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra cameras
  • New ministry laptop and the existing Legion volunteer editing setup
  • Replacement Legion charger and backup storage
  • Camera supports, audio, lighting, and charging equipment
  • A custom metal studio logo and set improvements
02

Sustain the Mission

Once immediate equipment needs have been met, additional donations sustain the work behind the resources.

  • Ongoing biblical teaching and video production
  • Development of devotionals and practical resources
  • Website, technology, and digital distribution costs
  • Administration and other charitable ministry expenses
  • Preparation for future ministry opportunities

Common questions

Giving with confidence.

Where does my donation go?

Contributions support VGM's charitable mission. The plan for this appeal prioritizes cameras, equipment, studio production and volunteer tools; contributions beyond immediate equipment needs help with ongoing ministry operations. We will adjust purchases according to actual funding.

Does my gift have to be made on December 1?

No. This campaign invites GivingTuesday and year-end support. The existing Donorbox form can also accept contributions outside GivingTuesday.

How can I verify Valiant Guardian Ministries?

Look up Valiant Guardian Ministries using EIN 41-2662681 in the official IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search. You may also contact us directly using the email address listed below. We do not request credit-card details over email or social media messages.

Can I give or volunteer in another way?

Yes. Contact VGM by email to discuss volunteering, contributing equipment, or other partnership opportunities.
