Faithful endurance is not built on anxious striving. It is built on the settled confidence that Christ is Lord, His Word is true, and His people are called to stand.

## Stand With Clarity

When Scripture speaks of armor, it is not inviting theatrical religion. It is calling believers to sober, practiced obedience.

- Truth steadies the mind.
- Righteousness guards the heart.
- Peace gives courage to move faithfully.

> The Christian life is not passive. It is patient, alert, and rooted in the promises of God.

Because of that, discipleship must be more than inspiration. It must form conviction, habits, and holy endurance.
