Devotional

Genesis 2

Devotional • Law • Genesis 2 • 2026-05-14 • Valiant Guardian Ministries

A devotional reflection on vocation, fellowship, and God's care in Genesis 2.

Genesis 2 slows the pace and brings us near to the personal care of God. He forms man from the dust, places him in the garden, and gives him meaningful work.

Reflection

Work is not a punishment in the biblical story. It is part of humanity’s calling before the fall. Fellowship is also not accidental. God declares that it is not good for man to be alone.

The chapter teaches us that faithful living includes both stewardship and relationship. We are called to tend what God entrusts to us and to honor the people He places beside us.

Prayer

Father, help us receive our work as service and our relationships as gifts. Form in us gratitude, responsibility, and love.
