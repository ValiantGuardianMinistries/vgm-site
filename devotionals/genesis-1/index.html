Devotional

Genesis 1

Devotional • Law • Genesis 1 • 2026-05-13 • Valiant Guardian Ministries

A devotional reflection on God's ordered, good, and sovereign creation.

God begins the story of Scripture by revealing Himself as Creator. The world is not random, and history is not drifting without purpose.

Reflection

Genesis 1 reminds us that order, beauty, and goodness begin with God. Every day of creation unfolds under His command, and everything He makes answers to His wisdom.

For the believer, that means our lives are not self-invented projects. We are creatures before we are achievers, and worship comes before control.

Prayer

Lord, teach us to receive Your world with gratitude and to live within Your order with humility, faith, and joy.
