Where Your Gift Goes

Your contribution supports the same VGM charitable mission described on our main Offering page, with two priorities for this campaign.

1. Production & Studio Development

Help equip our team to make and share more biblical teaching.

  • Three Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra cameras
  • A ministry laptop and volunteer editing setup
  • Studio logo, camera supports, lighting, and audio needs
  • Storage, charging, and other production accessories

2. Everyday Ministry Operations

Once immediate production needs are met, additional gifts will support ongoing VGM work.

  • Creating and distributing biblical teaching
  • Developing devotionals and discipleship resources
  • Technology and ministry administration
  • Long-term stability and ministry outreach

If less than the goal is raised, purchases will be prioritized based on funds available. Exact equipment and spending amounts may be adjusted to meet ministry needs. All gifts support VGM's charitable purposes, subject to any specific donor restrictions.
